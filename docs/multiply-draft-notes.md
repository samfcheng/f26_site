## Editorial choices

- Lead with the command center, Sam’s main project, followed by the CRM, emails, and reflections.
- Keep first-person language and the personal account of working with engineering, moving between projects, and learning about recency bias.
- Use the supplied answers and testimonials without inventing research findings or reasons for rejecting particular navigation options.
- Attribute the outcome to the combined dashboard, navigation, and CRM improvements. Separate contact volume (4×) from the proportion contacted within 24 hours (under 70% to approximately 95%). The comparison is the week before versus the two weeks after.
- Use direct excerpts from Paul and Madison, keeping Madison’s timeline estimate attributed to her.
- Keep the application/autofill work out of this first draft to focus the story on the projects with the most detail and supporting assets.
